## Philosophy

**1. The captures are the colour.** The UI is greyscale plus one purple accent. Images and videos bring the colour; the chrome around them stays quiet so they read first. Accent means "selected" or "this is the one"; status colours (warning orange, danger red, success green) mean exactly that and nothing else.

**2. Layers, not effects.** The screen is a stack of solid layers, told apart by fill lightness (higher is lighter in dark mode; in light mode the top layer is white and each lower one a step greyer) and 1px lines. No shadows, no blur, no glass, no translucent fills. One test decides a fill: *nothing that scrolls may look higher than what it scrolls under.* The clips scroll under the app bar, so the day labels that ride on them can't be lighter than the bar.

**3. Solid colours, see-through lines.** To soften a colour, shift its value; never fade it with opacity (`/80`). Lines are the one exception: they stay see-through so they blend with the fill under them.

**4. Nothing moves unless it means something.** No transitions or animations, except the few listed under Motion (the card hover reveal, the segmented pill, the toggle knob, the editor toolbars' fade, the History panel docking) and loading spinners. Hover states are instant colour changes (see Hover).

**5. Measure, then trust your eye.** Centre things on real measurements (the traffic lights, badge digits, the day label's gaps were all measured in the running app). Optical beats geometric: a "4" that's centred by its box still looks low, so it gets lifted. A line drawn outside a bar makes the bar read 1px taller and its contents off-centre, so edges are drawn inside. Spacing uses Tailwind steps only.

**6. Fewer things.** If nobody needs it, it goes: the capture count, the "Captures" title, file names on cards, the toolbar row. Tooltips are one word. Information that matters at a glance stays visible (the status bar), it isn't hidden behind an info button.

**7. Native macOS manners.** Arrow cursor on controls, a click never leaves a focus ring behind, Esc gets you out, the system's own overlay scroll bar, tools in the title bar like Finder, everything centred on the 52px the traffic lights sit in (the app bar is 53px: 52px plus its edge line).

**8. One rule, everywhere.** Roles, not raw colours. A thing that looks like a control is a control and looks like every other control of its rank. A badge always wears its icon's colour. The "filters" are one group (type filter, view switch, Select, missing files) and change together.

**9. Room for warmth.** Small, deliberate touches are fine where they cost nothing: the accent moon and sun, the sign-off in the bottom bar.

## Themes and roles

- **Dark** is the default (`@theme` in `src/index.css`); **light** applies under `html.light`. The toggle in the app bar switches them; on Windows the choice reaches the native title-bar controls via `titlebar:setTheme`.
- **Colour roles only** (`bg-elevated`, `text-fg-secondary`, `border-line`, …). Raw palette colours only for content: the annotation palette, background presets, things drawn onto a capture. Full table: `COLOR-ROLES.md`.
- **Fills are moving to three roles** app-wide: `elevated`, `toolbar`, `content`. The older ones (`background`, `surface`, `surface-raised`, `canvas`, `control`, `fill*`, the `.glass-*` classes) have no mounted user left since 2026-11-27 (the Dashboard's grid card was the last, on `control` / `canvas`): `background` stays for the window itself, the others only for the unmounted Share dialog and workflow picker and the Dashboard's removed launcher. The classes nothing used (`.card-organic`, `.glass-card`, `.titlebar`, `.titlebar-drag`, `.active-nav-bg`, `.primary-gradient-text`, `.no-scrollbar`) were deleted the same day.
- **Text ladder:** `fg` > `fg-secondary` > `fg-tertiary` > `fg-disabled` > `fg-faint`. `fg-faint` (#464646 / #c1c1c1) is decorative only, below every contrast minimum on purpose (the bottom-bar sign-off).
- **Status pairs:** each status has a pale fill and a text colour. Warning is orange, hue 27 (`warning-subtle` #421e00 / #ffead9, `warning-text` #e67a2a / #b95a00; dark was #ee9a56 until 2026-11-24, too light and pale beside `danger-text`). `danger-text` in dark is #e84b50, close to the solid `danger` red, so an icon and its badge read as one.
- **Accent: one colour in both themes** (2026-11-22): `#7440d5`, hover `#6931c8`, white on it (`on-accent`); the focus ring follows it. Dark mode had its own lighter accent (≈ #ab8eff, near-black on it) until then; a middle value (≈ #8a5fea) was tried. As an icon or text colour on the dark controls it's only ~2.9:1. `accent-subtle` / `accent-muted` (pale fills) stay per theme.
- **Hover colours** (2026-11-22): a fill has a hover one step on (`accent` → `accent-hover`; `kind-video` → `kind-video-hover`, 2026-11-27, the same OKLCH step, held equal by a test), and each coloured icon has a stronger hover value, one step past its rest colour (OKLCH L +.12 in dark, −.12 in light): `accent-text-hover` (≈ oklch .64 dark / #5410aa light), `danger-text-hover` (≈ #ff8480 / #970000), `warning-text-hover` (≈ #ffaa75 / #913500; it moves with its rest colour). An exact copy of the grey step (`fg-secondary` → `fg`) lands at near-white / near-black, so the step is shorter. Fills: `elevated-hover` (#282828 / #f4f4f4) and `toolbar-hover` (#212121 / #ececec), a step past `elevated` / `toolbar`.
- **Tooltip** (`tooltip`, `tooltip-ring`, `on-tooltip`) is dark in both themes.
- **Every window with UI follows the theme** (2026-11-24): the main window, the capture overlay, the recording toolbar, border and drawing layer get light / dark and the custom base (`useFullTheme` in `electron/index.ts`; the pooled overlays get the current theme at every open). They were fixed dark until then. Only the recorder host (no UI) has none.
- **Custom themes** (Settings › Appearance, 2026-11-22, `CUSTOM-THEMES.md`): the user picks an accent and a light and a dark base (presets or our own picker, which only offers colours that can be had). Every role is derived from the picks in OKLCH (`shared/theme-colors.ts`), within brightness bands per mode; the accent always takes white text. The windows above get the base tint and light mode too; the rest only the accent. Things drawn outside CSS (the annotation canvas's selection frame and delete button) read the roles at runtime (`hooks/useRoleColors.ts`).
- **One source for role values** (2026-11-27): `src/index.css`. Code never repeats a role's hex. Where CSS variables can't reach there is one copy each, kept equal by a test: `shared/theme-colors.ts` `DEFAULTS` (the theme maths needs numbers, and main reads its window colours from it; `tests/theme-colors.test.ts`), the status roles in `electron/browser-page.ts`, and the extension's fallbacks in `popup.html` and `background.js` (no build step to import with; `tests/extension-roles.test.ts`). Settings › Appearance draws its Default swatches from `DEFAULTS`: a custom theme overrides the variables, so `var(--color-background)` would put the user's own pick on the swatch named Default.

## Layers

The Dashboard's fills, highest first:

| Role | Dark / Light | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `elevated` | #1d1d1d / #ffffff | The app bar's buttons and the filters (raised a step above the bar), the bottom bar, a clip's selection circle |
| `toolbar` | #151515 / #f7f7f7 | The app bar, day labels, a clip's status bar and hover buttons, list rows |
| `content` | #0e0e0e / #f0f0f0 | The clip area |

Why the day labels and a clip's status bar sit on `toolbar`, not `elevated`: they scroll with the clips, under the app bar, so they mustn't look higher than it. The selection circle stays `elevated` (picked by eye). A hover action that can't run is hidden, not shown disabled (2026-11-21).

## Lines and depth

- **Three see-through line strengths:** `line-subtle` (.07 white dark / .10 black light), `line` (.12 / .14), `line-strong` (.22 / .22). Solid lines were tried and dropped.
- Where they go on the Dashboard: filters and Select `line`; day labels `line` (`line-subtle` until 2026-11-21); a clip's edge `ring-1 ring-inset ring-line` (`line-strong` while the card is hovered); its status bar's top edge `line`; hover buttons `line-strong`; a list row's action under the pointer `line-subtle`.
- **Bar edges are drawn inside the bar** (`.edge-b` on the app bar, `.edge-t` on the bottom bar), `line` in both themes (2026-11-21; light had `line-strong`, too strong). The bar adds the line's 1px to its height so its content is exactly centred in the space beside the line (2026-11-23): the app bar is 53px (52px + line, `pb-px`), the bottom bar 15px (line + 14px, `pt-px`); at 52px / 16px with the line inside, content sat 0.5px off. The lines are a 1px background stripe that stops 1px short of each side (2026-11-23; it was a full-width inset shadow), so it never stacks on the system's own see-through window outline (a darker / brighter dot at the ends). The bottom bar's line crosses the window's rounded corners, so its ends are masked along the corner's curve (radius 21.1px on macOS 27, set by eye; macOS doesn't tell web content).
- **Floating bars** (the editors' toolbars) draw their edge inside too: `ring-1 ring-inset ring-line`, so their 10px padding runs from the outer edge like the app bar's. So do the image editor's sidebars (History, Stickers).
- **Every edge sits inside its shape** (2026-11-26): `ring-inset` or a CSS border (border-box) on boxes; an SVG outline is a 2px stroke clipped to its shape, so only the inner 1px shows (a centred 1px stroke sticks out 0.5px and its mitred points further: the callout triangle's tip did). One exception: the video trim bar's filmstrip keeps an outset `line-subtle` ring (see Editors).
- **No shadows.** One exception, light mode only: `.light-lift-xs` (Tailwind `shadow-xs`) under the day labels, because white on near-white needs a hint of depth. Shadows that must paint over scrolling content need the content isolated (`isolate` on the clip area) so the content's own z-indexes can't climb over them.
- `box-shadow` used as a ring, an inset edge or the capture overlay's dim isn't a shadow.
- **Overlays stay inside borders** (2026-11-22): a layer laid over an element (the hover overlay, the missing cover) stops inside its border or edge ring (`inset-px` / radius minus 1px) and the edge stays on top, so the edge's colour never shifts.
- **Stacking** (2026-11-27, one scale for everything `fixed`): dialogs `z-[60]` (scrim and box), a dialog opened from a dialog `z-[70]` (the screen picker over the wallpaper preview), toasts `z-[80]`, callouts and menus `z-[100]`, a drag layer `z-[200]`, tooltips `z-[1000]`. So a dialog's scrim never dims a toast, and a callout opens over a toast. Inside a page, layers stay under 50 (the editors' bars 20, their sidebars 30, the selection rectangle 40). Before, dialogs sat at 50, 100 and 110: a toast showed over some and under others.

## Type

- **Manrope only** (exception in the system mono: badge numbers; the list rows' mono was dropped 2026-11-22), weights 300 to 800, self-hosted (`@fontsource/manrope`), set once as `--font-body` on `body`. Don't set `fontFamily` on elements; Konva text sets `Manrope, sans-serif` itself.
- Sizes in use on the Dashboard: 12px medium for control labels and day labels; 10px medium for the status bar; 10px semibold uppercase `tracking-wider` for text tags; 8px system mono semibold for badge numbers; 8px for the sign-off.
- **Type scale by rank** (2026-11-22). New text picks a rank; a label is never smaller than its description, and ranks that share a size differ by weight and colour.

  | Rank | Size / weight | Colour | Used for |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Logo | 21px extrabold | `fg` | App bar "Lumia" |
  | Section | 14px semibold | `fg` | Settings section heading |
  | Group | 12px semibold | `fg` | Day label |
  | Item | 11px semibold | `fg` | Settings row label |
  | Caption | 12px medium | `fg-secondary` | Settings group caption, the Dashboard's empty state |
  | Control | 12px medium | `fg-secondary` | Filters, view switch, Settings nav, buttons, select, keycaps |
  | Body | 12px regular | `fg-secondary` | A dialog's body text. An error line under a group is this size in `danger-text` |
  | Notes | 11px, 16px line | `fg` semibold title / `fg-secondary` regular body | Release notes |
  | Meta | 10px medium | `fg-secondary` | Grid status bar, Settings row description |
  | List | 11px medium | `fg` / `fg-secondary` | List row text, the editors' panel rows |
  | Badge | 8px semibold mono | | Count badge |
  | Toast | 12px medium `fg` / 11px medium `fg-secondary` | | Toast message / its reason line |
- `tabular-nums` where digits must line up (lengths and times in the status bar). Badge numbers use the system mono instead (2026-11-21), so a changing count keeps its width.
- Times are 24-hour only (`hourCycle: 'h23'`). Lengths carry units with a 1px gap before the unit ("20s", "1m 05s", "3h 32m"), so a length never reads like a clock time. Where a date goes with the time (list rows, 2026-11-22; Recent images): `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` with `tabular-nums` ("22 Nov 2026 10:31" tried). Recent images write "2h ago, 2026-11-23 13:43" (a week or older: the date and time alone).

## Spacing and radius

- **Tailwind steps only**, talked about as classes (`gap-2`, `pt-3`). No negative-margin tricks.
- **Related and unrelated** (2026-11-23): controls that belong together are **12px** apart (`gap-3`: the app bar's groups, the editor's tools · colours · size, undo · redo · clear · history), unrelated groups **40px** (`gap-10`: the app bar's page controls and its own buttons, the image toolbar's zoom and edit group). Groups pushed to opposite ends of a bar have no designed gap, only that 40px as a minimum. Other gaps: `gap-2` among a card's hover buttons, `gap-1` between tags, 6px between colour swatches, `gap-3` between clips, `gap-6` between a list row's columns.
- **Bar padding:** 10px from a bar's outer edge to its 32px controls: the app bar (10px above and below the buttons), the floating video and image bars (`p-2.5`), list rows (`h-[52px] px-2.5`).
- **Radius:** Tailwind's default scale (`rounded-md` 6, `-lg` 8, `-xl` 12, `-2xl` 16), capped at the macOS window corner: nothing inside the window is rounder than the window. Pills (`rounded-full`) are the exception.
- A corner radius is never larger than how far the element overhangs what it frames.

## Icons

- **Lucide** (`lucide-react` 1.77, pinned) through one component, `src/components/Icon.tsx`. Names are the old Material Symbols names mapped to Lucide in `ICONS`; add a mapping for a new name.
- Four sizes: 14px (tags), 16px (every icon button, the app bar, segments), 20px, 24px. One exception: the 12px pencil and × on a Custom swatch (24px circle; 14px filled too much of it). A second: the release notes' kind marks (sparkle, wand, bug, wrench) are 10px with a 0.75px line, as quiet as the bullets they replaced (2026-11-22).
- Line width is fixed in screen pixels (`.icon` + `non-scaling-stroke`): 1px at 14 / 16 / 20px, 1.25px at 24px, plus a 0.35px boost for dark icons on light grounds. Never pass `strokeWidth`. The boost keys off the theme; an icon on a colour of its own (the Custom swatch's pencil) sets its weight from that colour instead (`--icon-line` / `--icon-line-sm`).
- Hand-made icons in `Icon.tsx`: the filter stacks `ImagesStack` and `VideoStack` keep lucide 0.468's drawings (the 1.x redraw was tried and dropped); `RadioNarrow` (Uploaded) is Lucide's radio squeezed to 90% wide. `WindowMacTall` and `RectDashed` (the list's window and region icons) are Lucide's AppWindowMac and SquareDashed redrawn 20×18, the overall size of Lucide's Monitor. `MoonSlim` (the theme toggle's moon) is Lucide's moon with a bigger cut-out, so the filled crescent is about 10% slimmer (2026-11-21).
- The theme toggle's moon and sun are plain filter-button icons (2026-11-22; filled with the accent before).

## Buttons and controls

Details and ranks in `ICON-BUTTONS.md`. The short version:

- **Framed toolbar button:** 32px `w-8 h-8 rounded-lg bg-elevated border border-line`, 16px icon, `text-fg-secondary`. Used by the app bar's buttons, Select and the missing-files button.
- **Single select = `Segmented`, everywhere** (2026-11-23; the sticker tabs and the stroke picker were hand-built at other sizes): a `h-8 p-0.5 rounded-lg bg-elevated border-line` track, icon segments 28 × 26px with a 16px icon (or `content`: a stroke dot, an "A", a sticker thumbnail), text segments `px-2`. Selected: `bg-accent text-on-accent`, the purple of a selected clip's outline. The purple is one pill behind the segments that slides to the picked one (2026-11-22, `.segmented-pill-slide`: 160ms on position and width, no slide on first show, off under reduced motion). The white text follows the pill exactly (2026-11-23, iOS-style): the buttons are always grey and a white copy of the row on top is clipped to the pill's box, the clip sliding with it, so nothing turns white before the purple arrives (it flashed white-on-white in light mode). A value matching no option shows no pill. Options: `equal` (every segment as wide as the widest, like macOS: the History panel's tabs), `stretch` (fill the row). Two exceptions: colour swatches (circles with an accent ring: a fill can't sit behind a colour) and single-select grids (the next rule).
- **Single-select grids** (2026-11-27, Jasper; the stated exception to `Segmented`): a choice among framed options that needs more than one row, or whose options are cards, is a grid. The picked option is marked the way its kind is elsewhere: a card by the accent `--selection-ring`, a framed button by the accent fill with `on-accent`, which doesn't change under the pointer. One gap per grid: `gap-3` for a primary grid (the clips, the Browser picker's tabs, the screen picker's screens, the wallpaper photos), `gap-2` for a secondary one (Settings › Wallpapers' categories). A list of pages (the Settings nav, the Workflow list) is a column of 32px rows `gap-1` apart, the current one `bg-accent text-on-accent`. **Not covered:** the Background panel's 3×3 Alignment. It's a segmented track folded into three rows: one `border-line` track with `p-0.5`, 24px cells `gap-0.5` apart (78px square), the picked cell filled accent like a segment. It stays as it is (`gap-2` was tried for a few hours on 2026-11-27 and taken back; the rule is for framed options).
- **On / off state of a framed button** (2026-11-21, liked): the icon keeps its colour (2026-11-22; accent before: on/off icons stay neutral) plus a straight 3px `bg-accent` strip along the bottom edge, inside the frame and masked to its inner shape (`inset-0 overflow-hidden rounded-[7px]` wrapper) so it never covers the border at the corners. No fill. Used by Select in select mode and Settings on its page. Tried and dropped: a `bg-accent`-filled button (looked off beside the inset segmented filters), segments filled to their edge, the strip as an inset box-shadow (curves with the corners) or 8px in from each side (too short); heights 1 to 7px compared on a slider. One component since 2026-11-27, `components/OnStrip.tsx` (the markup was pasted into eight files): the button takes `relative` and `group/on`, and the strip steps to `accent-text-hover` under the pointer on every button (only the app bar's, Select's and the keycaps' did). It also marks Background, History, Filter, the image toolbar's callout buttons, Mic and Draw, and **Pin** on the History panel and the sticker sidebar (2026-11-27, Jasper: a framed button with the strip while pinned, one icon for on and off; it was bare when off and a filled box with a second icon when on).
- **Select** (last of the filters, 2026-11-22; second until then) is one button with three states, same frame in all: Select (`check_circle`, `fg-secondary`); in select mode with nothing selected the same check in the on state, tooltip "Cancel", which leaves select mode; with clips selected the red trash with a count badge (red at rest: one of the three exceptions under Delete buttons), keeping the strip, which deletes them (a delete keeps select mode). Until 2026-11-21 the none-selected state was a neutral `fg-secondary` ×, which nothing tied to the mode being on. Tried and dropped: a 20px "Select mode" banner and a 4px purple strip over the clip area, a separate Delete button, an accent × and CircleX. A click on empty space in the clip area leaves select mode (2026-11-22; from 2026-11-21 it only cleared the selection, like Finder); Esc leaves select mode. Delete / Backspace (also with Cmd) deletes the selected clips like the red trash; Cmd+A selects all of history under the type filter, also days not loaded, and turns select mode on (2026-11-25). Dragging in the clip area (in or out of select mode; a drag that starts select mode turns it off again if it selects nothing) draws an accent rectangle (`border-accent bg-accent/15`: see-through so the clips show, the one opacity modifier in the UI; the image editor's selection box uses the same one, 2026-11-24) that, like Finder, replaces the selection with every clip it touches (Shift-drag adds, Cmd-drag flips; the area scrolls near its top / bottom edge), and Shift-click works like Finder: the last plain-clicked clip is the anchor and stays, each Shift-click selects from it to the clicked clip and replaces the previous Shift-click's range, so clicking further down grows it and clicking back up deselects; a click that deselects drops the anchor, and a Shift-click without one ranges from the top of the clip's day (2026-11-21).
- **The app bar's Settings button** on the Settings page (2026-11-22) stays the gear in the on state; a click goes back to the Dashboard, tooltip "Close". **In the editors** (2026-11-23) it becomes the Back arrow to the Dashboard (`useAppBarClose`): the editor is a pop-over over the Dashboard, not a page of its own (an X "Close", a Back after the logo and a round X over the video were tried).
- **Colour swatches** (2026-11-25, every row: the image toolbar, the recording toolbar's drawing row, Settings › Appearance, the Background panel): **one look everywhere** (Jasper, 2026-11-27), `swatchClass` / `swatchFill` in `src/lib/swatch.ts`; no local variants. 24px circles, a see-through `line` edge (`line-strong` until 2026-11-25, now the buttons' edge) on an `elevated` base, the colour inside the border only (over the colour itself the edge all but vanished on saturated swatches), the pick a 1.5px accent ring 1px out (2026-11-27, Jasper; 2px before) whose offset is drawn in the row's ground (`toolbar`, or `elevated` inside a callout or the Background panel), 6px apart. The gap is part of the one definition too (`SWATCH_ROW`, `gap-1.5`; Settings' rows had sat 8px apart), as is how far the ring reaches (`SWATCH_RING_OUT`, 2.5px, for the Custom picker's callout). The colour picker's field and hue strip have the same base. **Custom** (`CustomSwatchIcon`): a grey + while there's no custom colour, the colour with a 12px pencil while it isn't in use (a click takes it back), the colour with a 12px × while it is (a click clears it and returns to the colour in use before; Settings: to Default or the preset). The pencil staying while in use was tried the same day.
- **Colour picker** (`ColorPicker`): the field over the hue strip. The strip's knob stays inside the strip (2026-11-25: its centre runs 6px in from each end; the hue picked is under its left edge at the start, its middle halfway, its right edge at the end). On the full-spectrum (`free`) picker only, the eyedropper (32px framed button, `colorize`, Chromium's EyeDropper) sits left of the strip, 8px from it, the strip shortened so the two match the field's width; theme pickers keep colours in a band, so they don't get it.
- **Sliders** (2026-11-25, the Background panel): a native range drawn flat (`.flat-range` in `index.css`): 4px `line-strong` track, the accent up to a 14px white knob with a `line-strong` edge; label left (Control rank), value right (11px `tabular-nums`, unit with no space: "5%").
- **Shared button classes** (`components/button-classes.ts`): `BUTTON` (framed text), `ICON_BUTTON` (framed 32px icon), `ACCENT_BUTTON` / `ACCENT_ICON_BUTTON` (the screen's main action, accent fill; disabled falls back to framed). Disabled: `text-fg-disabled`, no hover, never opacity. Since 2026-11-27 also `ICON_BUTTON_FRAME` (the 32px frame alone, for an icon with colours of its own: Select, missing files, the on-state gear), `ICON_LINK` (the same button on a link: its hover can't use `enabled:`), `DANGER_BUTTON` / `DANGER_ICON_BUTTON` (delete buttons, the next rule: `BUTTON` / `ICON_BUTTON` whose hover colour is `danger-text`) and `BARE_ICON_BUTTON` (a row's action). No file writes a framed button's classes out by hand.
- **Delete buttons turn red only under the pointer** (2026-11-27, Jasper, final): every button that deletes, discards, clears or disconnects rests in the grey of the buttons around it (`fg-secondary`) and shows its icon or label in plain `danger-text` under the pointer (`danger-text-hover` belongs to the three exceptions below). A card's and a list row's Delete, Clear edits and the recording's Clear and Discard (`DANGER_ICON_BUTTON`), Settings' Disconnect and the Workflow page's Delete and Remove (`DANGER_BUTTON` / `DANGER_ICON_BUTTON`). One that can't run is `fg-disabled` like any other button. **Three exceptions are red at rest** (`danger-text`) and step to `danger-text-hover` under the pointer: Select's trash in select mode, whose count badge wears the same red (`bg-danger-text text-danger-subtle`, following its icon to `danger-text-hover`); the armed "Confirm disconnect" (where there's no undo and no dialog, the button asks twice in place: Disconnect becomes "Confirm disconnect", red on `danger-subtle`, for 3s); and the trash on a selected shape in the image editor, drawn on the image. ## Keyboard and focus

- **Focus ring:** 2px `focus` purple (the accent), drawn inside the control (`outline-offset: -2px`) so it never overlaps a neighbour or gets clipped. In `@layer base`, so a utility can switch it off. A card's ring is an overlay over its thumbnail and status bar.
- **A mouse click never leaves focus** on a button, link or clip card (blurred after the click, like native macOS buttons); otherwise the next key press would light the ring on whatever was clicked last. Tab, Enter and Space keep focus as usual.
- **Esc** takes focus off the focused control (not in text fields). Clicks on the app bar can't, because they're window-drag clicks the page never sees.
- **A window becoming active** (from the menu bar, the Dock, a switch back) drops focus from a button, link or clip it landed on (2026-11-24: macOS could put it, ringed, on the first control). Text fields keep theirs. The capture overlay does the same at every open.
- **Dashboard keys:** the clip area itself is never a Tab stop. Arrows, Page Up / Down, Home / End scroll it from anywhere except text fields and dialogs. After arrow scrolling, Tab jumps to the first clip that's more than half in view (Shift+Tab: the last); scrolling by keys again takes focus off a clip. Clips and list rows are Tab stops; Enter / Space act like a click.

## Voice

- Plain, short, practical. Sentence case. No em dashes.
- **Terms** (2026-11-22): the things are **Image** and **Video**; both together are **files** ("No files yet", "Delete old files"), never "captures" or "clips" in the UI. The verbs are **capture** (images: "Capture region", "Click to capture this screen") and **record** (video: "Record region", "Stop recording"); never "screenshot" as a verb ("Screenshot region" was tried the same day and reverted). "Capture" never covers both (2026-11-27): where an action can be either, say "capture or recording", or follow the last kind, as the menu bar's **New capture** / **New recording** (capture when there's no last); no shared verb (Grab, Shoot, Take considered). Code keys (`'screenshot'`, `'recording'`) and the release notes keep their old words.
- Say what happens: "Clear 4 missing files from History", "Back to Dashboard".
- Progress ends in an ellipsis character ("Uploading…"). Tooltips one word.
- **Toast text** (2026-11-24; also the system notifications):
  - The outcome, past tense, sentence case, no closing period: "Image copied", "Link copied", "Saved to Downloads".
  - Names keep their capitals: Downloads (the folder), Drive, Lumia.
  - Name the thing when the verb alone is unclear: Image, Video, Link. Never "clipboard", "successfully", "screenshot".
  - No articles: "Couldn't remove trim", "Couldn't set wallpaper".
  - Two outcomes: "and" for one subject ("Trimmed and copied"), a comma when the subject changes ("Uploaded, link copied").
  - Failures start with "Couldn't": "Couldn't upload". The raw error goes on the second line, never into the message. Partly failed: the failed count of the total in parentheses, "Couldn't upload image (1 of 2)".
  - A wait ends in "…" with a spinner: "Downloading…".
  - A length in the app's units, in parentheses: "Video recorded (12s)", never a clock.
  - No em dashes, no " · ".
- **No dot dividers** (2026-11-24): don't join parts of a label with ` · `, it reads as machine-made. Use a comma, parentheses or a word: "Version 2.6.5, released 2026-11-18", "Video (press again to stop recording)". One exception: the clip card's status bar (time · source), where no alternative read better.
- Allowed warmth, once per screen at most: the sign-off.
